Common challenges

Where does money leak in music and entertainment?

The money is global. The banking usually is not, and a small cut comes out at every step.

Conversions

Paid in euros, converted twice

A fee paid in euros lands in a dollar or sterling account, then gets converted back to pay local crew and hotels.

Cost

The bank keeps a cut of every fee

At a 2% margin, $5,000,000 of international income costs $100,000 in conversions alone.

Timing

Budget at one rate, paid at another

Deposits go out at one rate and balances at another, so a tour that worked on paper loses money in practice.

Speed

Money stuck between territories

Slow international payments, fees on every receipt and accounts spread across several providers.
