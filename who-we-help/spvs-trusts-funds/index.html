Common challenges

Why do SPVs, trusts and funds struggle with FX?

The structure is the reason you hold the asset. It is also the reason most providers make currency harder than it needs to be.

Onboarding

Providers turn the structure away

Banks and FX platforms often decline trusts, SPVs and fund vehicles, or stall them in compliance for weeks.

Deadlines

The date is fixed, the rate is not

Completion dates, capital calls and distributions do not move. The exchange rate moves every second until you convert.

Size

Small rate gaps become big money

On a seven or eight figure transaction, half a percent on the rate is a five or six figure cost.

Governance

Every payment needs a clear trail

Trustees, directors and investors need to see which entity paid what, when, and at what rate.

What does the margin cost on a £10,000,000 asset?

A US business buying a UK asset for £10,000,000 through a vehicle. Same purchase, same day, two different margins.
