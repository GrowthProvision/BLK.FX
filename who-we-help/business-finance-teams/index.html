Common challenges

What FX problems do finance teams bring to BLK.FX?

Currency rarely shows up as one big problem. It shows up as a variance, a fee and a reconciliation headache, every month.

Budget

The rate moved after the budget was signed off

You set a budget rate, the currency moved, and now margin is down and the board wants to know why.

Cost

You cannot see what the bank is charging

The margin is built into the rate, so nobody can say what FX actually costs the business each year.

Admin

Month end takes too long

Payments across several banks and currencies, matched by hand, with fees that never quite reconcile.

Ownership

Nobody owns FX

Conversions happen on the invoice date by default, not because anyone decided it was the right time. Who owns FX in your business?
