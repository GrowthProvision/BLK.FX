04 / Protect

FX Risk Management & Hedging

Exchange rates move all the time. Hedging is simply a plan to reduce surprises by fixing some or all of a future rate, so you are not exposed later.

What is a forward contract?

A forward contract lets you lock in an exchange rate today for a future payment, so you know what you will pay or receive later.

Full protectionLock in all of a future amount.Partial protectionLock in some, keep some flexible.Spread it outCombine forwards and spot conversions to smooth the average rate over time.

Importing goods: lock rates so costs do not jump and margins stay protected. Paying tuition: lock a rate at the start of the year to avoid last-minute surprises.

Structured forwards can add flexibility when payment dates are not exact, up to a defined window.
