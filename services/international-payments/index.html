Payment rails

Which payment rail gets my money there fastest?

The route decides when a payment lands, not the fee on the invoice. We pick the fastest one for each country and currency, and use local clearing instead of SWIFT wherever a country has it.

01

Faster Payments

The UK's near-instant network. Sterling payments usually arrive within seconds to a couple of hours.

02

SEPA

Low cost across the euro zone, usually same or next working day. SEPA Instant settles in seconds where both banks support it.

03

SWIFT

Reaches almost any bank, in most currencies. The broadest coverage, but it can take one to five working days.

04

ACH

US domestic payments in dollars, low cost, typically within a couple of working days.

Common questions

International Payments, answered.

SWIFT reaches almost any bank in the world but routes through correspondent banks that can each take a cut and add a day or more. A local rail, such as Faster Payments, SEPA or ACH, settles inside that country's own clearing system, usually faster and without the correspondent fees.

It is usually a correspondent bank holding it in transit or a mismatch in the beneficiary's details. Our team traces the payment directly with the receiving bank and helps move it forward, rather than routing you through a support queue.
