Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is iFX Brokers?
iFX Brokers is a regulated online CFD broker that provides access to forex, indices, commodities, cryptocurrencies and share markets through MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5.

2. What is CFD trading?
CFD trading allows traders to speculate on whether the price of a financial instrument will rise or fall without owning the underlying asset.

3. What markets can I trade?
Trade Forex, Indices, Commodities, Cryptocurrencies and Shares from one trading account.

4. Does iFX Brokers offer MetaTrader?
Yes. Clients can trade using both MetaTrader 4 and MetaTrader 5 across supported devices.

5. What is leverage?
Leverage allows traders to control larger market positions using a smaller deposit. While leverage can increase potential returns, it also increases risk.

6. What are spreads?
A spread is the difference between the buy and sell price of a financial instrument and represents one of the costs of trading.

7. Is iFX Brokers regulated?
Yes. iFX Brokers is an authorised Financial Services Provider and regulated OTC (ODP) provider in South Africa.
